For the teacher

22 students. 25 minutes.

Welcome

Stops use video time. These are suggested teaching pauses, not Church-assigned edits. Both complete videos are included.

Video 1 · 3:08

After the discussion of Jesus Christ and forgiveness. Write a few words in box 1.

Video 1 · 7:21

After the discussion of hymns, prayer, and focusing on Christ. Choose one thing to try; use box 2.

Video 2 · 2:33

After the teacher demonstrates sharing with a partner. Give nearby pairs 45 seconds; use box 3.

Before students arrive

  • Print the handout: 11 copies, single-sided, at actual size. Cut each sheet in half for 22 cards. Bring pencils.
  • Place cards on chairs. If needed, ask two helpers to pass them out at the first video stop.
  • Make 11 pairs with people sitting beside each other. If attendance is odd, use one group of three.
  • Test sound and save both videos through Video options. A downloaded copy works without streaming; use the linked file so captions and pause points match.

Keep the pace

Ask for two or three short responses, not an answer from everyone. Students can speak, write, draw, or pass. Use this page’s Fullscreen button so pause prompts stay on screen.

Extras & activities
