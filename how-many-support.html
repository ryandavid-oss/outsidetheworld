Start with Apple Health

how many supports iPhones running iOS 27 or later. Record a cycling workout on your watch or cycling app and enable that app's sharing with Apple Health. Connect Health in how many, then pull down on Today to check for rides. You can explore a clearly fictional sample journal before connecting.

Garmin and other devices

An Apple Watch is not required. On a Garmin watch, enable Apple Health in Garmin Connect under More → Settings → Connect Apps, then sync with Connect open in the foreground. Open how many after the workout reaches Health.

Garmin documents limited initial history, no GPS-route transfer and no detailed workout heart-rate trace through this connection. An account with only an Edge cycling computer does not offer the Apple Health connection. Distance and calories depend on what the source supplies. Missing fields stay unavailable. See Garmin's setup instructions. Direct Garmin cloud sync and FIT/TCX file import are not supported.

Missing rides or numbers

Check that the workout is marked Cycling in Health, that the recording app has finished syncing and that how many has permission to read the relevant types. Keep your iPhone unlocked during the initial import. A paused import keeps saved pages, and interrupted Health reads resume after unlocking while the app is active.

Settings → Re-read all accessible rides retries missing details and preserves your notes. Health permissions and source-app history limits can hide dates or details. No rides visible does not prove that Health is empty. Totals describe available imported history.

Repeated recordings

Two apps can write separate workouts for one outing. Settings → Review repeated rides helps you inspect matching entries. Open a ride and turn off Include in totals for a copy you want left out. The choice is reversible and applies across progress, Archive, analysis and widgets. The workout stays in Journal with its notes and photos, and Apple Health is unchanged.

Make it yours

Settings lets you choose miles or kilometres, edit your bicycle details and pick your favourite food comparisons. Units apply to summaries, charts, cards and widgets. Food equivalents are approximate energy arithmetic, not dietary guidance. Chicken McNuggets and other named products belong to their respective owners; no sponsorship is implied.

Keep a memory

Open a ride to add a note or choose photos. Manual selection works without connecting your whole library. Photo suggestions are optional and you confirm each link. Archive brings together available years, places, records and memories.

Manually selected photos are stored as private resized copies. Suggested photos remain links to the originals. Library access changes and unavailable iCloud photos can affect what you see; originals remain in Photos.

Sharing

Review the card and edit its caption before opening the ordinary iOS share sheet. Choose whether to include calories or a photo. The optional frgmnts handoff opens a draft, and you decide whether to publish. Neither path shares exact route locations, heart rate or private notes automatically.

Local storage and recovery

Your journal is excluded from device backup. There is no journal backup or restore feature in this release. Reconnecting Apple Health can restore accessible workouts, but it cannot restore notes, bike details, counting choices, photo links or private photo copies after the app or journal is deleted. Copy important notes and keep original photos before erasing.

Erase this local journal removes the open journal's local additions. Apple Health, original Photos and cards already shared elsewhere are unchanged. Read the app privacy policy before sharing sensitive details.

Understanding the analysis

Cycling Fitness compares suitable sections of your own familiar rides and explains what is missing. A single ride receives no fitness verdict. These are unvalidated personal trends, not power, VO₂ max or medical measurements. Forecasts require an internet connection; saved journal data stays available without one.
