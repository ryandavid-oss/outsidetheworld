What I believe

Tuesday, February 4, 2025

I believe in the restoration of the gospel of Jesus Christ. Essentially, I am a believing member of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints because of that simple, profound fact...

I have learned how to listen to the Holy Spirit confirm truth to me in my life, heart, and mind. I love being part of the body of Christ and feeling the love and compassion of a ward family.

So please, be patient with me as I share my experiences with you through this dedicated account. I challenge each of you to walk with me with an open heart and listen to my story...

Frequently Asked Questions

How can someone be transgender and a member of the Church?

Gender identity is a deeply personal and complex aspect of who we are... For me, and for many others, being transgender is a part of our identity, just like being gay or lesbian is part of someone else's.

"Some people feel their inner sense of gender does not align with their biological sex at birth... The Church does not take a position on the causes of these feelings." — Church Website
How does your identity fit within the Plan of Salvation?

Quite simply, it doesn't appear that it fits at all. Like many aspects of faith, interpretations vary. Many transgender Latter-day Saints find peace in the expansion of understanding...
