# SUPER FRGMNTS // Arrival on Veyra

Status: Approved canon opening for Season One // Veyra and Episode 01 //
The Shard Foundry.

## Dialogue

### D01 // DRAS EHDRE

You came.

### D02 // ARYN SOL-MAVI

Aryn Sol-Mavi. You must be Dras Ehdre.

### D03 // DRAS EHDRE

I am. I was beginning to think no one beyond Veyra had heard us.

### D04 // ARYN SOL-MAVI

We heard you…eventually. Your signal was wrapped in an Expra-7 trisyllabic
cipher. It took three weeks to authenticate.

### D05 // DRAS EHDRE

Plain speech wouldn’t have made it off-world.

### D06 // ARYN SOL-MAVI

Excuse me?

### D07 // DRAS EHDRE

Never mind. I hoped the Fleet would send more than one ranger.

### D08 // ARYN SOL-MAVI

They classified Veyra as a Class Six containment risk. No deployment.

### D09 // DRAS EHDRE

Veyra supplied half the Arca System with Vesperite. If that doesn’t qualify as
a priority, the Fleet has forgotten what keeps its lights on.

### D10 // ARYN SOL-MAVI

They haven’t forgotten.

> The ground trembles. Dust slips from the outpost canopy. Aryn studies the
> signal on her wristpad.

### D11 // DRAS EHDRE

Then why did I wait twenty-seven days for one ranger to arrive?

> Aryn disconnects her wristpad from Central Command. Its cyan signal goes
> dark.

### D12 // ARYN SOL-MAVI

Because the Fleet rejected your call. I was in the room when they buried it.

### D13 // DRAS EHDRE

And yet here you are.

### D14 // ARYN SOL-MAVI

On my ship. On my authority. Central doesn’t know I landed.

### D15 // DRAS EHDRE

Then you’re braver than I thought. Or maybe you’re just terrible at following
orders.

### D16 // ARYN SOL-MAVI

Those aren’t mutually exclusive.

### D17 // DRAS EHDRE

No. I suppose they aren’t.

### D18 // ARYN SOL-MAVI

Start at the beginning. What happened beneath the Coreworks?

### D19 // DRAS EHDRE

Eighteen months ago, a drill team in the Deepworks broke into a seam that
wasn’t on any survey. Communications failed. Our Seam Integrity team went down
to investigate. Then the entire shift disappeared. More than two hundred women
and men. Gone.

> Aryn’s expression hardens. She lets the silence remain.

### D20 // DRAS EHDRE

We sent team after team to investigate. Only one person made it back to the
portal alive. What they described was clear: something had infested the
Coreworks. By then, the atmospheric stabilizers had been destroyed.

### D21 // ARYN SOL-MAVI

Is it contained below the surface?

### D22 // DRAS EHDRE

For now. But the air below is lethal, and whatever killed those workers is
still moving through the facility.

### D23 // DRAS EHDRE

When production stopped, so did the Vesperite payments. The economy collapsed
in a week. Anyone who hadn’t already left took what they could and found work
off-world.

### D24 // ARYN SOL-MAVI

I’m sorry for your people. But you’re asking me to enter a poisoned facility
after something erased two hundred workers. If you want my help, we speak
plainly.

### D25 // DRAS EHDRE

Disobedient and wise. Who knew?

### D26 // ARYN SOL-MAVI

Apparently, just what Veyra needs. So we’re agreed. Tell me everything you
know.

### D27 // DRAS EHDRE

You’re probably wondering why I stayed. The failures weren’t random. Before
each one, the same pulse moved through the Vesperite seams. I don’t think this
is an accident. I think it’s a response.

### D28 // ARYN SOL-MAVI

A response? You make it sound like something down there is answering us. Whose
response?

### D29 // DRAS EHDRE

That’s what I’ll pay you to find out. If you accept—and if you survive—there
are entire crates of Galactic Credits below. Whatever you recover is yours.

### D30 // ARYN SOL-MAVI

My mind was made up before the credits. But I won’t say no to honest money.

### D31 // ARYN SOL-MAVI

Where do I start?

### D32 // DRAS EHDRE

The Foundry. Bring both atmospheric stabilizers online and clear the
infestation. The pulse fractures Vesperite along its path. Recover the
fragments—their residual harmonics may tell us where it began.

### D33 // ARYN SOL-MAVI

And the portal?

### D34 // DRAS EHDRE

It still opens.

> Dras looks toward the dormant portal.

### D35 // DRAS EHDRE

It just hasn’t brought anyone back.

### D36 // ARYN SOL-MAVI

Then let’s improve its record.

> The Coreworks portal begins to ignite.

## Canon holds

- The sole survivor remains alive but otherwise undefined for a later Veyra
  episode.
- Neither Aryn nor Dras names or understands the Resonant in this scene.
- The interruption and interception threat remain deliberately unresolved.
- The eight-minute Foundry timer begins only after Aryn crosses the portal.
- Stage directions are performed visually; they are not rendered as dialogue
  cards or announced as spoken dialogue.
