USE CASE
Production-oriented environment outpaint for a side-scrolling 16-bit platform game.

INPUT ROLES
Image 1 is the edit target: a 1672 × 1882 expansion canvas. Its transparent
upper half is the new generation area. The original 1672 × 941 room begins at
y=941.

Image 2 is a structural reference only: a collision guide showing required
walkable edges. Do not reproduce its grid, labels, typography, colored bars,
route lines, legend, translucent overlays, or interface styling.

PRIMARY REQUEST
Extend the subterranean science-fiction room upward by one complete screen.
Create a vast explorable industrial gantry with grated steel catwalks,
maintenance platforms, steel mezzanines, trussed service bridges,
pipe-supported ledges, suspended machinery, vertical support towers, open
shafts, and ventilation chambers.

PRESERVATION CONTRACT
Change only the transparent upper plate and the blend zone from y=941 through
y=1120. Preserve rows y=1121 through y=1881 exactly. Do not redraw, relight,
rescale, crop, stretch, or reinterpret the protected original plate.

SEAM REQUIREMENTS
Continue major pipes, columns, rocks, cables, lights, shadows, and structural
supports through the y=941–1120 blend zone so the old/new boundary disappears.
Create at least two visually clear openings from the original room into the
new gantry.

STYLE
Strict orthographic side view. Crisp, intentionally pixelated 16-bit game
background art at the same pixel scale as Image 1. Match its lighting,
shadows, palette, rock formations, steel architecture, cyan crystals, magenta
energy, vegetation, and atmospheric depth. Retain generous navy-black negative
space. No perspective shift, isometric construction, antialiasing, painterly
blur, vector-smooth edges, or photorealism.

COLLISION STRUCTURE
Use Image 2 only to locate required horizontal walkable top edges. Cyan and blue
surfaces above y=1121 should become believable steel architecture with visible
support beneath: trusses, brackets, columns, hangers, or pipe stanchions.

Normal route elevations are 128px apart. Keep neighbouring platforms visually
separate and preserve open space around them for a 112px-tall character.

The room-transition structures at y=580 must reach x=0 and x=1672 exactly so
adjacent rooms align. Keep their outer ends perfectly flat and undecorated
across the canvas boundary.

Pink geometry is an optional future jump-boost route. It must never be required
to complete either normal path.

Anything shown below y=1120 is collision annotation only. Do not generate
platform artwork in the protected original plate.

READABILITY
Keep the playable silhouettes unmistakable. Maintain dark negative space
around platforms so Aryn Sol-Mavi, enemies, projectiles, Signal Shards, hearts,
and future powerups remain readable. Use background machinery at lower contrast
than foreground walkable edges.

AVOID
No characters, enemies, weapons, collectibles, powerups, text, letters,
numbers, signs, logos, interface elements, guide labels, grids, colored guide
bars, route lines, fake exits, diagonal walkable floors, floating unsupported
platforms, tiny unusable ledges, lighting discontinuities, or watermarks.

ROOM-SPECIFIC ADDITIONS

FOUNDRY
Heavy furnaces, magnetic cranes, suspended casting machinery, reinforced steel
frames, magenta energy conduits, cyan mineral deposits, restrained heat haze,
and soot-darkened catwalks. Preserve the existing DNA-like containment tower as
a visual anchor without copying it into the upper room.

REFINERY
Dense pipe forests, pressure tanks, pumping stations, exhaust vents, amber
safety lights, valve towers, maintenance bridges, and oily grated service
decks. Keep the center readable rather than filling every void with pipes.

BIOLAB
Glass growth chambers, containment bridges, dripping pipes, bioluminescent
plants, teal and magenta organic contamination, observation platforms, and
sealed research machinery. Organic growth may wrap supports but must not cover
walkable top edges.

UPLINK
Signal relays, antenna machinery, cable towers, luminous data conduits,
suspended transmitter arrays, an open central ascent space, and increasingly
advanced infrastructure leading toward the Uplink Gate. Do not add a second
gate or fake exit.

ACCEPTANCE CHECK
1. The final composite remains 1672 × 1882.
2. Rows y=1121–1881 are pixel-identical to Image 1.
3. Required walkable edges above y=1120 are crisp and horizontal.
4. The y=580 room links reach both canvas edges exactly.
5. No grid, labels, guide bars, or route lines appear in the artwork.
6. No character, collectible, enemy, UI, logo, text, or watermark appears.
