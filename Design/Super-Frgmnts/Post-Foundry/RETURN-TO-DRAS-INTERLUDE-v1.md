## Purpose

The interlude must accomplish six things:

1. pay off Dras's warning that the Coreworks portal had never brought anyone
   back;
2. make the recovered specimen the engine of the next story rather than a
   detached reward;
3. distinguish the deeper Primary Biolab from the Biolab processing floors
   Aryn already crossed in The Shard Foundry;
4. make the Coreworks transport failure a consequence of the return and the
   specimen rather than a random delay;
5. introduce the inside of Aryn's RD-42 as a reusable, character-bearing
   sanctuary; and
6. send Aryn below again with a stronger mystery and the first restrained hint
   of the Fleet cover-up.

This is a playable breath after a major fight. It is untimed, contains no
combat, and does not grant an immediate backpack or weapon upgrade.

## Canon holds

- The recovered material remains **Wound-touched Vesperite**.
- Aryn knows it belongs to the Vesperite family but does not understand its
  altered state, stored pulse, purpose, or origin.
- The specimen remains secured in Aryn's backpack. Dras scans it without
  confiscating, consuming, or installing it.
- Neither Aryn nor Dras names or understands the Resonant during this
  interlude.
- The specimen participates in the transport overload. The deeper cause of the
  overload remains unresolved.
- The visible repair is real: one phase coupler and two shielded field braids
  are destroyed.
- A hidden Fleet interlock, deliberate sabotage, or other concealed layer may
  later explain why the overload became destructive, but none of those
  explanations is canonized here.
- The destination is the **Primary Biolab**, a sealed level below the
  production-facing Biolab processing floors already crossed in Episode 01.
- The sole survivor remains alive but otherwise undefined.
- Dras remains weathered, dry, observant, and guarded. He is not converted
  into a villain or an exposition terminal.
- Characters may continue to say **portal**, matching the approved opening
  dialogue. System copy and design documentation use **Coreworks transport**
  for the physical machine and its energy-vortex transit field.
- The interlude does not decide final rifle persistence, ammunition, weapon
  balance, store behavior, or the permanent backpack-upgrade system.
- Whether this sequence closes Episode 01, opens Episode 02, or bridges them
  remains a release-structure decision. Its story order is locked independently
  of that packaging choice.

For this interlude only, Dras waits at the Coreworks Threshold after detecting
the first successful Uplink return.

- Aryn uses the existing surface-return spawn near the transport.
- Provisional Dras post-return anchor: **world x = 6,090**, feet at **y = 744**.
- Aryn's existing return spawn begins at approximately **world x = 6,316**.
- The Coreworks transport remains centered at approximately
  **world x = 6,548**.

## Playable sequence

### Beat 1 // The first return

Aryn materializes on the surface beside the Coreworks transport. The vortex
collapses normally behind her.

Presentation:

- no mission clock;
- no completion music continuing beneath the scene;
- restored atmospheric lights remain visible in the distance;
- surface ambience returns after a short quiet hold;
- Dras is readable west of the transport before dialogue begins; and
- the objective remains `RETURNED TO VEYRA // REPORT TO DRAS`.

The moment is relief without celebration. Aryn survived, but the specimen's
close cyan-violet emission keeps the encounter unresolved.

### Beat 2 // Dras scans the specimen

Dras uses his existing field probe to perform a noncontact scan while the
specimen remains in Aryn's pack.

The scan establishes only the following:

- it is Vesperite;
- it retains a repeating pulse after separation from the seam;
- its stored charge is not decaying normally; and
- surface instruments cannot resolve the signal structure.

Dras explains that the production-facing Biolab decks Aryn crossed were
processing floors. The Primary Biolab below them retained:

- pre-collapse Vesperite assays;
- a phase spectrograph;
- sealed drill-team samples; and
- deeper laboratory records isolated from the production network.

The beta objective becomes:

`WOUND SPECIMEN // PRIMARY BIOLAB SIGNAL`

The eventual repaired-transport objective remains:

`WOUND SPECIMEN // REACH PRIMARY BIOLAB`

### Chapter 01 close // The signal answered

For the beta chapter close, the inert Coreworks transport answers the
specimen before Aryn touches the controls. Dras confirms that the response
came from below the Primary Biolab. A full-stage closing card holds:

`VEYRA // CHAPTER ONE COMPLETE`

`THE SIGNAL ANSWERED`

The player deliberately returns to the surface after reading the card. This
does not consume the specimen, activate the transport, or begin Chapter 02.
The Chapter 02 planning seed lives at
[`../Chapter-02/VEYRA-CHAPTER-02-PLAN-v1.md`](../Chapter-02/VEYRA-CHAPTER-02-PLAN-v1.md).

### Beat 3 // The transport answers

After the conversation, Aryn approaches the Coreworks transport and uses the
normal Down/interact action from its deck.

The first post-return activation does not transport her. It runs a dedicated
failure sequence:

| Time | Event |
| --- | --- |
| 0.00–0.30 s | The physical transport wakes with its normal cyan field. |
| 0.30–0.72 s | The field begins acquiring a Primary Biolab route. |
| 0.72–1.02 s | The specimen emits one violet-electric-blue pulse from Aryn's pack. |
| 1.02–1.34 s | Violet energy contaminates the cyan transport vortex. |
| 1.34–1.58 s | The phase coupler fails; both field braids arc and burn open. |
| 1.58–2.20 s | The vortex collapses, restrained sparks fall, and one dead component ejects into the housing tray. |

The failure:

- cannot damage Aryn or Dras;
- cannot throw Aryn from the deck;
- freezes actor input and stows the heavy rifle;
- keeps Dras visible in the scene;
- does not use a hidden random chance;
- does not consume the specimen;
- does not restart the mission timer; and
- completes in a deterministic state.

Reduced-motion presentation shortens the sequence, removes camera shake and
rapid flashes, and communicates the cyan-to-violet contamination through a
single color hold, a visible fault icon, system text, and an accessibility
announcement.

Required announcement:

> Coreworks transport fault. Transit field collapsed. Aryn is safe.

### Beat 4 // Dras identifies the repair

Dras examines the failed housing after the field is inert.

The required replacement set is:

- one **phase coupler**;
- two **shielded field braids**; and
- their shared keyed transit-service case.

Aryn carries a compatible Fleet-spec service kit aboard the RD-42. Dras asks
for the complete case rather than loose parts so the repair cannot fail for
want of a minor connector.

The objective becomes:

`COREWORKS TRANSIT FAILED // RECOVER RD-42 SERVICE KIT`

During this objective:

- the main-story objective points directly to the RD-42 hatch;
- the hawk remains autonomous ambience and never indicates objectives;
- the surface remains untimed;
- post-return calibration hazards remain inactive; and
- no enemies repopulate the Overworld.

### Beat 5 // Enter the RD-42

Aryn returns to Landing Flats, climbs the approved ship silhouette, stands on
the top-middle roof perch, and presses Down at:

`▼ ENTER RD-42`

She physically descends through the dorsal hatch. The complete boarding,
interior, and exit contracts live in
[`../Overworld/Phase-3/Ship/Interior/RD42-SHIP-INTERIOR-CONTRACT-v1.md`](../Overworld/Phase-3/Ship/Interior/RD42-SHIP-INTERIOR-CONTRACT-v1.md).

Inside the ship:

- the service kit is the only mandatory interaction;
- the cockpit, habitation details, Trillian berth, and pack bench are
  optional;
- the specimen may provoke a restricted partial match from the offline Fleet
  index;
- the pack bench does not install or unlock an upgrade; and
- weapons remain stowed.

Recovering the case sets:

`SERVICE KIT RECOVERED // RETURN TO DRAS`

The main-story objective then points directly to Dras at the Coreworks
Threshold.

### Beat 6 // Collaborative repair

Aryn returns to Dras with the complete case and uses:

`▼ DELIVER SERVICE KIT`

The repair is a short authored environmental beat rather than an inventory
menu or wiring puzzle.

Provisional sequence:

1. Aryn places the service case beside the transport housing.
2. The case opens to show one coupler and two separately keyed braids.
3. Dras installs the phase coupler.
4. Aryn connects the two braids to the upper field terminals.
5. Dras closes the housing and initiates a low-power test.
6. The transport returns in cyan.
7. One restrained violet pulse crosses the field in sympathy with the
   specimen.
8. The field stabilizes instead of collapsing.

The repair must show Aryn and Dras contributing. Aryn does not merely hand over
the quest object and watch Dras solve the problem alone.

### Beat 7 // Descent to the Primary Biolab

The restored system reports:

`DESTINATION LOCKED // PRIMARY BIOLAB`

The objective becomes:

`TRANSPORT RESTORED // DESCEND TO PRIMARY BIOLAB`

The interlude ends when Aryn deliberately boards the repaired transport. No
automatic transition removes her from the surface at the end of dialogue.

The final visual hold is the familiar cyan field carrying a thin violet-blue
harmonic. That image promises the next level without explaining it.

## Dialogue presentation

- Use Field Relay for all Aryn/Dras cards.
- Use Coreworks Archive presentation for transport faults, destination text,
  and optional RD-42 computer output.
- Return dialogue pauses the simulation and hides gameplay touch controls.
- Failure dialogue begins after the environmental animation; the failure
  itself is not described by dialogue cards.
- Repair stage directions play in the world while actor input is locked.
- Every card follows the existing Continue, Skip, focus, reduced-motion, and
  accessibility contract.
- The conversation camera must keep the living world visible behind the
  dialogue rather than replacing it with a full-screen lore panel.

## Accessibility and failure recovery

- The complete interlude is operable with shared actions on keyboard, touch,
  and future controllers.
- Focus loss pauses dialogue, field animations, repair animation, audio, and
  actor control together.
- Transport overload and repair cannot leave Aryn in a half-transition state.
- On reload, the scene restores to the last completed deterministic state:
  before failure, after failure, after kit recovery, or after repair.
- Reduced motion replaces rapid energy movement, shaking, and long descent
  motion without removing cause-and-effect information.
- Cyan and violet communicate atmosphere, but fault and success states also
  use shape, text, sound, and accessible announcements.
- Missing critical ship-interior or transport assets produce a visible retry
  path rather than a blank scene.

## Greybox acceptance

The interlude is ready for visual production only when:

- the existing surface-return route carries the specimen reliably;
- Dras appears at the threshold only in the post-Wound state;
- the return dialogue, failure, ship objective, repair, and departure occur in
  the documented order;
- Aryn cannot bypass the failure and enter the Primary Biolab early;
- the service kit cannot be collected twice;
- the main-story objective points to the correct target in both directions;
- the Western Signal Flats remains optional exploration with no objectives;
- the route passes at desktop and 390 × 844 portrait-mobile framing;
- focus loss cannot strand the transport or ship transition; and
- there are no console errors, missing critical assets, or contradictory
  prompts.
