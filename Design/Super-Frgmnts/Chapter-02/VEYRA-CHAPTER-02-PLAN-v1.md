# SUPER FRGMNTS // Veyra Chapter 02 Plan v1

**Status:** Preproduction seed

**Established:** 2026-07-28

**Working title:** The Primary Biolab

**Entry condition:** Aryn has restored both atmospheric stabilizers, defeated
Seam Hunter, recovered the Wound-touched Vesperite, returned to Dras, and
witnessed the dormant Coreworks transport answer the specimen.

## Chapter promise

Chapter 01 asks whether Aryn can survive the abandoned Coreworks. Chapter 02
asks why the Coreworks—and possibly the Fleet—recognize what she brought back.

The player descends below the production-facing Biolab floors into the sealed
Primary Biolab. This is not a second Foundry. It is a quieter, more deliberate
place where observation, containment, and biological systems replace heavy
industrial machinery.

The chapter must preserve three mysteries:

1. why Wound-touched Vesperite retains and transmits a pulse;
2. what answered Aryn's pack from below the Primary Biolab; and
3. why an offline Fleet-era system still recognizes the signal.

The sole Coreworks survivor remains alive but undefined. The Resonant remain
unnamed and unexplained.

## Player-facing pillars

1. **Read before acting.** Every new hazard gives a safe observation window
   before it demands execution.
2. **One new verb, reused deeply.** The specimen enables a provisional
   resonance-reading interaction. It reveals relationships; it is not a
   universal key or instant solution.
3. **Biology changes machinery.** Doors, lifts, and conduits respond through
   living growth, pressure membranes, assay light, and fluid routing.
4. **Easy-to-medium precision.** Failure costs position or one health unit,
   not long repeated traversal.
5. **Story through space.** The lab's containment logic should communicate the
   research program before an archive explains it.

## Provisional level spine

| Area | Teaching purpose | Primary play |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Transit Intake | Reorient after the cliffhanger | Safe arrival, broken decontamination lock, first resonance read |
| Assay Galleries | Teach signal relationships | Pair specimen pulses with matching assay chambers |
| Containment Nursery | Combine movement and ecology | Timed membrane openings, low-threat Chitin encounters, safe recovery alcoves |
| Records Vault | Turn mastery into discovery | Route power while avoiding archive purge sweeps |
| Primary Core | Invert the learned rule | The signal responds before Aryn initiates the final assay |

The loop is:

`observe → test safely → perform once → combine with movement → invert for reveal`

No room should introduce a second unexplained rule before the first has been
demonstrated without punishment.

## Difficulty envelope

- Standard gaps remain within the Chapter 01 normal-jump safety margin.
- Required rises use visible intermediate landings; jet-assist creates
  optional shortcuts rather than mandatory blind recovery.
- Moving-platform transfers provide at least 0.8 seconds of readable overlap
  at the intended boarding point.
- Hazard cycles show one complete safe-warning-active cadence before the
  player must cross.
- Checkpoints occur before the first combined ecology/hazard room and before
  the chapter encounter.
- Chitin Sentinels remain the recognizable combat baseline. New enemies are
  introduced one family at a time and never simultaneously with a new
  traversal rule.

## Story beats

1. The repaired or rerouted transport reaches an intake that should have no
   active destination lock.
2. The Wound-touched Vesperite opens a non-networked assay path without being
   removed from Aryn's pack.
3. Lab records establish that Primary Biolab researchers were studying pulses
   before the collapse.
4. Evidence implies that at least one system continued operating after the
   staff disappeared.
5. The final chamber responds to Aryn's presence or credentials in a way that
   cannot yet be explained.

## Chapter 02 closing options

These are options, not canon:

- **Expected arrival:** an offline terminal displays Aryn's ranger identifier
  before she authenticates.
- **Living archive:** the lab answers in the cadence of the recovered
  specimen, then repeats a fragment of Dras's original distress signal.
- **Survivor trace:** a recent manual override proves someone moved deeper
  after Chapter 01 began.

Choose one closing reveal. Do not stack all three.

## Future platform backlog

### Desktop fullscreen

Use the browser Fullscreen API behind a deliberate user action. Preserve the
1672 × 941 logical canvas, letterbox rather than stretch, let Escape exit
fullscreen before it pauses, and restore focus to the game shell. This is a
quality-of-life feature, not a Chapter 01 beta blocker.

### Controller support

Use the Gamepad API with:

- left stick and D-pad for movement;
- south face button for jump;
- west face button or right trigger for fire;
- north face button for weapon switch;
- down/D-pad down for contextual interaction;
- Start for pause;
- a configurable deadzone and reconnect handling; and
- prompt glyphs that change only after confirmed controller input.

Controller support must pass the same dialogue, pause, focus-loss, and
portrait-safe interaction contracts as keyboard and touch. It should be
prototyped early in Chapter 02, then backported to Chapter 01 after the mapping
is stable.
