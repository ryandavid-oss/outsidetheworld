What happened to 2002? Honestly?

Well, it was a great trip to Utah. With no high expectations, I was able to do everything I wanted to do and more. The leaves were still in full fall spectacle, and I was able to drive in the snow (that was great fun).

And guess what: I have pictures. Yes, you can find those under the Media portion of my site...I had to keep two of them at full size. Hint, those one's make great desktop wallpapers.

Under Outside the World, you will find:

The Wedge is my daily writing, scribble, blog, or whatever you'd like to call it. I haven't quite finished the archiving feature, so you may find some really random looking pages.

Media This is where I throw all of my pictures. Unfortunately, i haven't had the time or resources to place anything there yet. But check back often!

In Writings you'll find a book of poetry and a shameless short story. The short story is a continuing work, and you'll have to check back weekly for updates on the progress of the action.

You'll find various things in Links. Probably just links to old sites of mine, outdated pages, and archives.

As always, you can Contact me. I promise to write back in a timely manner.

Please take some time and visit the other web sites, too. You'll find helpful news and tips about technology and other writings at the Tech Desk.
