For the teacher

Ages 16–17. 25 minutes.

Welcome

Stops use video time. These are suggested teaching pauses, not Church-assigned edits. Both complete videos are included.

Video 1 · 3:08

After the discussion of forgiveness. Give everyone 30 quiet seconds to write an idea about Christ in section 1.

Video 1 · 7:21

After the discussion of worship. Ask what could help someone participate when it feels difficult; use section 2.

Video 2 · 2:33

After the partner demonstration. Share a question or where to begin looking for an answer; use section 3.

Before students arrive

  • Print the handout: one card per student. Each page makes two cards; print single-sided at actual size and cut in half. Bring pens.
  • Place cards on chairs so the first pause can stay quiet. If needed, pass them out at that stop.
  • Use pairs with people sitting near each other. If attendance is odd, use one group of three.
  • Test sound and save both videos through Video options. A downloaded copy works without streaming; use the linked file so captions and pause points match.

Make room for honest answers

Ask what students noticed before explaining what you noticed. A question can stay open at the end of class. If you do not know an answer, say so and identify a scripture or reliable Church source to explore together. Invite personal reflection; do not collect the cards or ask anyone to describe private mistakes.

These choices follow the Church’s guidance on inviting learners to ask questions, ponder, and act.

Keep the pace

Allow a few seconds of silence before inviting answers. Take one or two responses and ask a follow-up such as “What makes that difficult?” Students may speak, write, or pass. Use this page’s Fullscreen button so pause prompts stay on screen.

Psalms extra

Use only if time remains. Read Psalm 119:105, then explore one phrase. The Hebrew title of Psalms, Tehillim, means “praises.” Ask what a lamp reveals and what it leaves unseen. Let students consider an application without making a promise about when they will receive an answer.
