# SUPER FRGMNTS — Aryn and Dras Dialogue

This document contains the two Aryn/Dras dialogue encounters currently implemented in `super_frgmnts.html`.

The card IDs (`D01`, `R01`, and so on) map directly to the game runtime. Please preserve them while revising. Italicized stage directions are shown to the player; italicized runtime cues trigger presentation or environmental effects.

## Encounter One — First Contact on Veyra

**D01 · DRAS EHDRE** — Coreworks Foreman
Greetings.

**D02 · ARYN SOL-MAVI** — Signal Ranger
You must be Dras Ehdre.

**D03 · DRAS EHDRE** — Coreworks Foreman
I am. I was beginning to think no one beyond Veyra had heard us.

**D04 · ARYN SOL-MAVI** — Signal Ranger
We heard you…The fleet classified Veyra as a Class Six containment risk. No deployment. The Fleet rejected your call. I was in the room when they buried it.

**D05 · DRAS EHDRE** — Coreworks Foreman
And yet here you are.

**D06 · ARYN SOL-MAVI** — Signal Ranger
I have my own ship, and my authority. Central Comm doesn’t know I landed.

**D09 · DRAS EHDRE** — Coreworks Foreman
Veyra supplied half the Arca System with Vesperite. If that doesn’t qualify as a priority, the Fleet has forgotten what keeps its lights on.

**D10 · ARYN SOL-MAVI** — Signal Ranger
*Stage direction — VEYRA // SUBSURFACE TREMOR: Dust slips from the outpost canopy.*
*Runtime cue: tremor*
That's why I'm here. What happened inside the Coreworks?

**D19 · DRAS EHDRE** — Coreworks Foreman
Eighteen months ago, a drill team in the Deepworks broke into a new seam. Not part of any survey. It was an incredible find. Days later, communications failed. Our Seam Integrity team went down to investigate. Then the entire shift disappeared. More than two hundred women and men. Gone.

**D20 · DRAS EHDRE** — Coreworks Foreman
We sent team after team to investigate. Only one person made it back to the portal alive. What they described chilled us all to the bone: something had infested the Coreworks. By then, the atmospheric stabilizers had been destroyed.

**D21 · ARYN SOL-MAVI** — Signal Ranger
Is it still contained below the surface?

**D22 · DRAS EHDRE** — Coreworks Foreman
For now. But the air below is lethal, and whatever killed those workers is still moving through the facility.

**D23 · DRAS EHDRE** — Coreworks Foreman
I am desperate for your help, Aryn. The entire economy collapsed on Veyra. Anyone who hadn’t already left took what they could and found work off-world.

**D24 · ARYN SOL-MAVI** — Signal Ranger
I’m sorry for your people. But you’re asking me to enter a poisoned facility after something erased two hundred workers.

**D25 · DRAS EHDRE** — Coreworks Foreman
There are entire crates of Galactic Credits below. Whatever you recover is yours.

**D26 · ARYN SOL-MAVI** — Signal Ranger
I won’t say no to honest money.

**D27 · DRAS EHDRE** — Coreworks Foreman
Aryn, these infestations aren't random. There is a pulse moving through the Vesperite seams. I don’t think this is an accident. I think it’s a response.

**D28 · ARYN SOL-MAVI** — Signal Ranger
A response? Whose response?

**D29 · DRAS EHDRE** — Coreworks Foreman
That’s what I’ll pay you to find out.

**D30 · ARYN SOL-MAVI** — Signal Ranger
My mind was made up before the credits.

**D31 · ARYN SOL-MAVI** — Signal Ranger
Where do I start?

**D32 · DRAS EHDRE** — Coreworks Foreman
The Foundry. But not with your pack in its present state. Half the old routes are sealed behind plating and collapsed decks.

**D33 · ARYN SOL-MAVI** — Signal Ranger
My beam was designed not to pass through bulkheads. Most crews consider that a feature.

**D34 · DRAS EHDRE** — Coreworks Foreman
The survey rigs used a phase lattice called Ghost Vector. It let a cutting beam slip through dead material before it resolved. I kept the pattern and enough lattice grain for one installation.

**D35 · ARYN SOL-MAVI** — Signal Ranger
You want to rebuild my pack before I step through your homicidal portal.

**D36 · DRAS EHDRE** — Coreworks Foreman
I want to give you the material and let you rebuild it. Your ship has a cleaner bench than anything left here.

**D37 · ARYN SOL-MAVI** — Signal Ranger
Good answer. I trust my own hands.

**D38 · DRAS EHDRE** — Coreworks Foreman
*Runtime cue: look toward portal*
Install Ghost Vector aboard the RD-42. Then bring both atmospheric stabilizers online, recover the twelve loose fragments, and follow their pulse to its source.

**D39 · ARYN SOL-MAVI** — Signal Ranger
*Runtime cue: prime portal*
Hold the portal. I’ll bring it a better gun.

## Encounter Two — Return from the Coreworks

**R01 · DRAS EHDRE** — Coreworks Foreman
You came back.

**R02 · ARYN SOL-MAVI** — Signal Ranger
True to my word. Though your portal did most of the work.

**R03 · DRAS EHDRE** — Coreworks Foreman
It has never done that before.

**R04 · ARYN SOL-MAVI** — Signal Ranger
You could sound happier...

**R05 · DRAS EHDRE** — Coreworks Foreman
I've lived long enough to stop expecting good news.

**R06 · DRAS EHDRE** — Coreworks Foreman
Please, tell me what you found. The ground has been shaking for hours.

**R07 · ARYN SOL-MAVI** — Signal Ranger
Both atmospheric stabilizers are online. The Foundry and Biolab processing floors are breathing again.

**R08 · DRAS EHDRE** — Coreworks Foreman
What put them offline?

**R09 · ARYN SOL-MAVI** — Signal Ranger
A Seam Hunter. It was nesting over a geological rupture.

**R10 · DRAS EHDRE** — Coreworks Foreman
A Seam Hunter? Here on Veyra?

**R11 · ARYN SOL-MAVI** — Signal Ranger
It won't break another relay.

**R12 · ARYN SOL-MAVI** — Signal Ranger
*Stage direction — WOUND SPECIMEN // SECURED: Aryn turns enough for the cyan-violet material in her pack to become visible.*
*Runtime cue: reveal specimen*
This was beneath the Seam Hunter when it fell.

**R13 · DRAS EHDRE** — Coreworks Foreman
That's not Vesperite.

**R14 · ARYN SOL-MAVI** — Signal Ranger
I gathered that.

**R15 · DRAS EHDRE** — Coreworks Foreman
No. True vesperite lies still once it leaves the seam.

**R16 · ARYN SOL-MAVI** — Signal Ranger
This one doesn't. And it's hot as hell.

**R17 · DRAS EHDRE** — Coreworks Foreman
*Stage direction — DRAS FIELD PROBE // NO MATCH: The probe repeats the specimen's pulse but cannot resolve its signal structure.*
*Runtime cue: scan specimen*
It's holding a pulse without losing charge.

**R18 · ARYN SOL-MAVI** — Signal Ranger
Do you still have the diagnostic equipment necessary to analyze this material?

**R19 · DRAS EHDRE** — Coreworks Foreman
Not here. The Primary Biolab kept the old assays.

**R20 · ARYN SOL-MAVI** — Signal Ranger
I passed through a biolab.

**R21 · DRAS EHDRE** — Coreworks Foreman
You crossed its processing floor. The main laboratory is below it.

**R22 · ARYN SOL-MAVI** — Signal Ranger
Sealed?

**R23 · DRAS EHDRE** — Coreworks Foreman
No longer accessible from production. But the portal can take you there.

**R24 · ARYN SOL-MAVI** — Signal Ranger
Then I go back.

**R25 · DRAS EHDRE** — Coreworks Foreman
You say that as if the first trip went well.

**R26 · ARYN SOL-MAVI** — Signal Ranger
It went forward. I danced with the Seam Hunter, and won.

**R27 · DRAS EHDRE** — Coreworks Foreman
Something tells me you're looking for a few more dances.

**R28 · ARYN SOL-MAVI** — Signal Ranger
You might say that.

**R29 · DRAS EHDRE** — Coreworks Foreman
Aryn. Be careful. If there's a true Seam Hunter on Veyra, it's not alone.

**R30 · ARYN SOL-MAVI** — Signal Ranger
Dreams do come true.

**R31 · DRAS EHDRE** — Coreworks Foreman
Then let's get you ready. You'll need all the help you can get.
