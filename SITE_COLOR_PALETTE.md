## Recommended Usage

- Default to `Core OTW` first.
- Use `Homepage Accents` for flagship moments, not everywhere.
- Use `Warm OTW Accents` for memory, intimacy, tribute, or personal/editorial surfaces.
- Use `Christina Gee Resume Palette` only for her page unless you intentionally expand that design system.
- Use archive neutrals as support, not as lead colors.

## Avoid

- Using too many accent families on one page.
- Letting muted grays replace your actual brand colors.
- Reintroducing heavy dimming with dark overlays when contrast can do the job more cleanly.
- Mixing the sage resume palette into core OTW pages without a deliberate reason.
