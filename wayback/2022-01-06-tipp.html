The iPhone Photo Project

With the right circumstances, photography that comes from our phones can be striking. With this in mind, I started The iPhone Photo Project.

Every picture you see here comes from an iPhone 4 or 4S. The app of choice is Hipstamatic, a wonderful, inspiring invention that adds character and depth.
