Look more closely

Binding up a wound

The image is of someone tending an injury. Isaiah goes on to describe ruined towns being rebuilt. The chapter reaches from personal grief to the life of a whole community.

Jesus says the passage is fulfilled.

Jesus reads from Isaiah and declares the scripture fulfilled in His listeners’ hearing. By identifying Himself with the Spirit-anointed messenger, He claims the mission of bringing God’s promised restoration.
