# SUPER FRGMNTS — Revised Level Dialogue

Runtime transcription of every active Aryn/Dras dialogue encounter in **Arrival on Veyra**. Speaker names, card IDs, spoken copy, and authored stage/effect cues match `super_frgmnts.html`.

> **Scope:** Active story dialogue only. The retired `legacyReturnDialogueCards` deck is intentionally excluded because the current episode progression never presents it.

## Encounter 1 — Arrival on Veyra

*Initial meeting with Dras; Ghost Vector assignment.*

**D01 · DRAS EHDRE** — Coreworks Foreman  
Thank the maker. I thought nobody was coming.

**D02 · ARYN SOL-MAVI** — Signal Ranger  
You must be Dras Ehdre.

**D03 · DRAS EHDRE** — Coreworks Foreman  
I am. And you are?

**D04 · ARYN SOL-MAVI** — Signal Ranger  
Aryn Sol-Mavi.

**D05 · DRAS EHDRE** — Coreworks Foreman  
Where is everybody else? We are barely containing the disaster below.

**D06 · ARYN SOL-MAVI** — Signal Ranger  
CentCom buried your request. I was in the room when it happened. They don't consider this a priority.

**D07 · ARYN SOL-MAVI** — Signal Ranger  
I came in my own ship, and my authority is my own. No one knows I'm here.

**D09 · DRAS EHDRE** — Coreworks Foreman  
Veyra supplies half the Arca System with Vesperite! If that doesn’t qualify as a priority, the Fleet has forgotten what keeps its lights on.

**D10 · ARYN SOL-MAVI** — Signal Ranger  
*Runtime cue: Effect: `tremor` — VEYRA // SUBSURFACE TREMOR — Dust slips from the outpost canopy.*  
That's why I'm here. What happened inside the Coreworks?

**D19 · DRAS EHDRE** — Coreworks Foreman  
Eighteen months ago, a drill team in the Deepworks broke into a new seam. Not part of any survey. It was an incredible find. Days later, communications failed. Our Seam Integrity team went down to investigate. Then the entire shift disappeared. More than two hundred women and men. Gone.

**D20 · DRAS EHDRE** — Coreworks Foreman  
We sent team after team to investigate. Only one person made it back to the portal alive. What they described chilled us all to the bone: something had infested the Coreworks. By then, the atmospheric stabilizers had been destroyed.

**D21 · ARYN SOL-MAVI** — Signal Ranger  
Is it still contained below the surface?

**D22 · DRAS EHDRE** — Coreworks Foreman  
For now. But the air below is lethal, and whatever killed those workers is still moving through the facility.

**D23 · DRAS EHDRE** — Coreworks Foreman  
I am desperate for your help, Aryn. The entire economy collapsed on Veyra. Anyone who hadn’t already left took what they could and found work off-world.

**D24 · ARYN SOL-MAVI** — Signal Ranger  
I’m sorry for your people. But you’re asking me to enter a poisoned facility after something erased two hundred workers.

**D25 · DRAS EHDRE** — Coreworks Foreman  
There are entire crates of Galactic Credits below. Whatever you recover is yours.

**D26 · ARYN SOL-MAVI** — Signal Ranger  
I won’t say no to honest money.

**D27 · DRAS EHDRE** — Coreworks Foreman  
Aryn, these infestations aren't random. There is a pulse moving through the Vesperite seams. I don’t think this is an accident. I think it’s a response.

**D28 · ARYN SOL-MAVI** — Signal Ranger  
A response? Whose response?

**D29 · DRAS EHDRE** — Coreworks Foreman  
That’s what I’ll pay you to find out.

**D30 · ARYN SOL-MAVI** — Signal Ranger  
My mind was made up before the credits.

**D31 · ARYN SOL-MAVI** — Signal Ranger  
Where do I start?

**D32 · DRAS EHDRE** — Coreworks Foreman  
The Foundry. But not with your weaponry in its present state. Half the old routes are sealed behind plating and collapsed decks.

**D33 · ARYN SOL-MAVI** — Signal Ranger  
My weaponry was designed not to pass through bulkheads. Most people consider that a blessing.

**D34 · DRAS EHDRE** — Coreworks Foreman  
I have an idea. The survey rigs used a phase lattice. They nicknamed it "The Ghost". It let a cutting beam slip through material like butter. I have enough lattice grain for one more implementation.

**D35 · ARYN SOL-MAVI** — Signal Ranger  
You want to rebuild my pack before I step through your homicidal portal?

**D36 · DRAS EHDRE** — Coreworks Foreman  
I want to give you the material and let you rebuild it. Your ship has a cleaner bench than anything left here.

**D37 · ARYN SOL-MAVI** — Signal Ranger  
Good answer. I trust my own hands.

**D38 · DRAS EHDRE** — Coreworks Foreman  
*Runtime cue: Effect: `look-portal`*  
Take the grain and get to work. Return to your ship and I'll work on getting this portal ready for you.

**D39 · ARYN SOL-MAVI** — Signal Ranger  
*Runtime cue: Effect: `prime-portal`*  
On it.

## Encounter 2 — Emergency Extraction

*After Aryn survives the first Seam Hunter encounter.*

**S01 · DRAS EHDRE** — Coreworks Foreman  
Aryn? Your signal was fading. Thank Zarquon I locked onto you. What happened?

**S02 · ARYN SOL-MAVI** — Signal Ranger  
Quick. Seal the portal first.

**S03 · DRAS EHDRE** — Coreworks Foreman  
*Runtime cue: Effect: `look-portal`*  
Already done...Tell me what the hell happened!

**S04 · ARYN SOL-MAVI** — Signal Ranger  
There's a Seam Hunter in the Coreworks.

**S05 · DRAS EHDRE** — Coreworks Foreman  
*Runtime cue: Effect: `tremor` — COREWORKS THRESHOLD // SILENCE — For once, Dras has no answer ready.*  
A Seam Hunter? Here? They don't nest in worked seams. They empty them.

**S06 · ARYN SOL-MAVI** — Signal Ranger  
This one has made itself comfortable. My beam barely marked its armor.

**S07 · DRAS EHDRE** — Coreworks Foreman  
This is worse than I imagined.

**S08 · ARYN SOL-MAVI** — Signal Ranger  
Send me back. Give me a Diet Coke and send me back.

**S09 · DRAS EHDRE** — Coreworks Foreman  
You're insane. And Diet Coke doesn't exist on Veyra.

**S10 · DRAS EHDRE** — Coreworks Foreman  
What you need is a bigger gun. Our crews had a thermal extender made for cutting pressure-vessel scars from the inside. Split the beam, seed every ray, and the Hunter's own armor will hold the heat against it.

**S11 · ARYN SOL-MAVI** — Signal Ranger  
That does sound better than Diet Coke.

**S12 · DRAS EHDRE** — Coreworks Foreman  
The bad news is, you'll need material. The catalyst is in a quarantined materials vault three levels deep. Clinical storage area. Cleaner than the Foundry, but not empty.

**S13 · ARYN SOL-MAVI** — Signal Ranger  
Get the portal ready.

**S14 · DRAS EHDRE** — Coreworks Foreman  
Aryn, you barely escaped with your life.

**S15 · ARYN SOL-MAVI** — Signal Ranger  
This isn't a request. I'm dancing with that Seam Hunter again.
