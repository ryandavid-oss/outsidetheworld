Humid Amber Tone

August 16, 1999/RyanDavid Burningham
The humid amber tone accented by the touch of the forest light captures the picture-perfect memory of summer and a child's passion for mischief. So weak are the times and fleeting the moments when memories are made, like the tick of the last second on my old silver watch. One must remember this when the quest is begun so the purpose is not lost. We all must find ourselves sometime. I prefer to find myself in a happier place.
