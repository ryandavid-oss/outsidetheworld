The Inside of Stress

[RECOVERED_SIGNAL]/RyanDavid Burningham
Is there ever room for change? Or could you say it expands as it goes along? Does it destroy everything it touches or improve its surroundings? It depends. What do you see, from where are you standing? From the inside you see the stress. From outside you see the beauty. Take your point of view and remove it from you. Now— how do you see the world? Has it changed you yet or have you tried to change yourself?
