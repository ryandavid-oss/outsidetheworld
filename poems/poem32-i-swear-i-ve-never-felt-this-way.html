I Swear I've Never Felt This Way

[RECOVERED_SIGNAL]/RyanDavid Burningham
I swear I've never felt this way how many feelings can one mind produce? caught away I suppose in the train of thought that grasps me. I contemplate the real challenge That I might be changing from 1 to 2 or it could be vice-versa. But I can only listen to understand feel to experience and hold on to love. Again I swear I have never felt this way but maybe you have.....
