Treasures Unlocked

[RECOVERED_SIGNAL]/RyanDavid Burningham
Time passes away, leaving treasures unlocked, and misery and hope mingle together in a fine jam. When you hear life's triumphant chime, you can almost taste the sweetness and bitterness is swept away in a sea of tranquility. As I open my mind and let the cool breeze of joy fill my heart, it is then that I am close. It is then that it feels real. It is then that I am able to feel the gracious touch of divinity that has far flown from my hands and lost itself in a far star-laden plain where only dreams fear to tread its awesome depths with their armor of hope. Where only wishes can tarry and there wait in patient hiding till the time comes that all can be done, and the dreams and hopes of a little boy can show themselves to the world.
