Effervescent Streets

January 15, 1998/RyanDavid Burningham
The sun breaks out and soars over the effervescent streets, courses its way through the clouds which have for so long hidden its glory from the land. The snow glistens underneath its new-found friend. Smiling, it seems, it has new life and it begins again to lead another. As the streets flood with water, my eyes fill with tears. Where will my tear fall? Where will the flood go?
