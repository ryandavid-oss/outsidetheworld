The Mistake of Depth

May 3, 1999/RyanDavid Burningham
I listened to her words tonight and was confused with their meaning. The deepness of them was their mistake and it capitalized on my heart. So I heard them all in succession, one by one till they were finished. And it made me wonder. I could have sworn I've heard it before. The rhetoric is killing me. But old words were given new skin and it made me wonder. So now I lay here quietly thinking upon the events, giving each thought specific attention. And I'm not giving up. Because it capitalized on my precious heart.
