Sonoran Vitality

November 11, 1998/RyanDavid Burningham
Summer rain, warm drops of water dancing across the street, cooling the choking summer heat with every single tap. Thunderclap races across the desert, echoes through the mountains, shakes the ground on which the cactus grow with every single burst. Lightning bolt illuminates the dark solemn sky, streaks across the heavens, draws a picture with its claws with every single spark. Summer rain.
