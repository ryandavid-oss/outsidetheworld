Missing 11:30

April 4, 1999/RyanDavid Burningham
Lacking the time for rational thought, I look at my watch to see what I've got. 11:45! says the band so cheery. I guess I have missed 11:30. What happened to time, why did it leave me? What did I do to miss 11:30? I guess I was tired or, maybe just sleepy, but nothing denying I've missed 11:30. So here is my question, answer me clearly: What would you do if you'd missed 11:30? I must leave you dangling and dripping with thought. I must check my watch now to see what I got.
