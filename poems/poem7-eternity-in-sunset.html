Eternity in Sunset

November 7, 1998/RyanDavid Burningham
If I could describe a summer sunset, what words would I choose? If I could make the world a better place, where would I start? If I could go anywhere in the world right now, why would I go there? If I could choose just one person to spend eternity with, who would I choose? If I could have the three wishes of my heart granted, how would I do that? And if I could choose a moment to begin to live, when would that be?
