Couch with a Name

December 13, 1998/RyanDavid Burningham
It's 8:35 and I'm feeling quite uncreative. So I decide to make something to eat. I eat my food still feeling uncreative. So I figure why not create something? Great idea! says the couch in the corner, the one that hasn't been sat on in a large quantity of time. So you take his advice and give him a name, and he liked it because it was creative.
