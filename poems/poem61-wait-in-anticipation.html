Wait in Anticipation

[RECOVERED_SIGNAL]/RyanDavid Burningham
I know what tomorrow will bring, it's an advantage that I have. I tend to enjoy it, the future holds within my hope for a new day. And each new day, will pace itself only for me. Cast off worries, for it will come and days will pass as effortlessly as the sands which count its life. I can sense a new eagerness. It livens my soul. And I close my eyes now; tomorrow will find them open again. Bright and shining, I wait in anticipation...
