Clouds in Reflection

[RECOVERED_SIGNAL]/RyanDavid Burningham
Listen to your mind. Hear the familiar tune. Conquer your distant fears and walk along life's path with ease. And when you see the clouds swimming along a watery blue sky, reflect them back upon the mountains and the passages in your life. The moon will disappear and with little effort your clouds will show they have some semblance of reality.
