The scriptures

The presentation uses selected phrases from the King James Version. Read the full passages alongside them.

  • Isaiah 61 — the anointed messenger’s work of healing, release, and comfort.
  • Luke 4:16–30 — Jesus’ declaration and His neighbors’ response.
  • 1 Kings 17:8–24 — Elijah and the widow at Zarephath.
  • 2 Kings 5:1–14 — Naaman’s healing through Elisha.
  • Leviticus 25 — the Jubilee background to release and return.
  • This week’s Come, Follow Me study — October 12–18, 2026, Isaiah 58–66.

Further reading

  • Anathea Portier-Young on Isaiah 61 — the chapter’s social and material setting.
  • Ruth Anne Reese on Luke 4 — the sequence of the conversation and hometown expectations.
  • Judith Jones on Luke 4 — release, Jubilee, and help beyond familiar boundaries.
  • Joan Taylor, What Did Jesus Wear? — evidence and interpretation concerning clothing.

The pictures and The Chosen

The Nazareth painting is based on the scene in The Chosen, season 3, episode 3. The widow and Naaman pictures are imagined scenes inspired by the show’s visual direction.

The show’s dialogue helps bring the scene to life. Luke records Jesus’ declaration of fulfillment and the confrontation that follows. The show’s statement about being the Law of Moses is added dialogue.
