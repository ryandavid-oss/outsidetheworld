This week’s lesson

This week’s lesson asks how we can trust the Lord and recognize His wisdom in everyday life. Proverbs offers practical counsel about our choices, our words, and our relationships. Ecclesiastes acknowledges that life can still be painful and uncertain. Together, they invite us to follow Jesus Christ, stay willing to learn, and give our attention to what lasts.

THE SCRIPTURES

In plain language

Proverbs

Listen to good counsel. Trust God. Watch your words, be honest, care for others, and accept correction. Wisdom takes practice in the choices we make every day.

Ecclesiastes

Life is brief, and much is beyond our control. Success and possessions cannot make everything meaningful. Receive everyday joys as gifts from God, do good, and remember Him through life’s changing seasons.
