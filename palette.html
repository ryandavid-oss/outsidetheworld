Typography by surface

Hierarchy before font inventory

OTW editorial

The public site’s current rhythm: direct sans, literary serif, and quiet system notation.

Interface and body

System Sans

Fast, familiar, and deliberately neutral across navigation and public-facing copy.

A quarter-century of digital residue keeps finding its way back to the signal.

Use for: navigation, body copy, controls, and compact utility text.

Editorial voice

Georgia

A grounded, human serif for major titles, dek copy, and reflective editorial moments.

Returned signal from the sleeping mind.

Use for: page titles, article framing, pull quotes, and authored moments.

Metadata

System Mono

A restrained operational layer for labels and signal language without over-styling the page.

LIVE_NOW // OTW_SIGNAL ARCHIVE_STATUS // OPEN

Use for: section labels, timestamps, coordinates, and small readouts.

frgmnts product

A precise product voice for the private social app and its companion web surface.

Product sans

IBM Plex Sans

Calm, structured, and authored enough to distinguish the product from generic interface typography.

A private place for the people and moments you choose to keep close.

Use for: app shells, navigation, settings, and structured product copy.

Product mono

IBM Plex Mono

The compact technical counterpoint used for labels, tokens, and small product states.

FRGMNTS // PRIVATE SEAT_STATUS // READY

Use for: chips, metadata, launcher language, and compact controls.

Supporting voices

Purposeful alternates for campaigns, archives, poetry, and moments that need a distinct temperature.

Supporting sans

Saira

A compact, technical voice that adds focused energy without replacing either primary system.

The signal moves beyond the expected.

Use for: display accents, event language, concise headlines, and energetic moments.

Warm display

Fraunces

Intimate, devotional, and image-led for Word.person and poetry-adjacent surfaces.

A room made of light, paper, and wanting.

Use for: emotional display copy, poetry titles, and companion editorial lanes.

Archive serif

Merriweather

A quieter literary voice for archive reading and longer reflective passages.

The archive does not just remember; it re-frames, softens, and returns.

Use for: archive reading, literary bodies, and slower text.

Campaign sans

Inter

A clean neutral option for utility-heavy or externally partnered surfaces.

Clear language leaves more room for the work itself.

Use for: campaigns, utilities, and contexts where neutrality is the priority.

Signal mono

Fira Code

Use for: broadcast metadata, technical framing, and live signal language.

Supporting serif

A human note inside the machine.

Use for: structured editorial intros and authored supporting moments.
