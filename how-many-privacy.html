how many / Privacy

Your journal stays with you.

how many is a local cycling journal operated by Outside the World is New, LLC. No account, advertising or analytics. We do not receive your journal.

Effective September 30, 2026.

Local means local.

Imported workouts, notes, bike details, photo links, private photo copies and analysis are excluded from this app's device backup. There is no journal backup or restore feature. Reconnecting Apple Health can restore accessible workouts, but cannot restore your local additions after erasing the journal or deleting the app.

Apple Health

You choose which data how many may read: cycling workouts, distance, active energy, heart rate and recorded routes. Optional recovery context uses sleep, resting heart rate and heart-rate variability. A Readiness score can be entered manually. These details support your journal and personal comparisons on your iPhone. The app does not write to Apple Health, send your journal to us, sell health data or use it for advertising.

Change access in the Health app's Apps and Services settings. Permissions and available history can limit what appears. Revoking permission stops future access but does not erase previously imported local copies; use the journal's erase control to remove those.

Photos and memories

You can choose individual photos with Apple's picker without giving how many access to your whole library. The app saves a private resized copy of a manually selected image with original camera and location metadata removed. If you connect optional suggestions, the app checks accessible photo capture times and locations against your rides on this iPhone. You confirm every link. Suggested photos remain references to your library, and may become unavailable if access changes.

Your original Photos are never changed or deleted. Apple may download a selected iCloud photo. Photos permissions remain under your control in iPhone Settings.

Maps, weather and Apple services

Apple Maps supplies map imagery for recorded routes. The weather feature requests your approximate current location only when you ask for a forecast, and sends that location to Apple Weather. It does not track your location in the background. Forecasts may be cached locally. Apple services operate under Apple's privacy policy.

Widgets

The app and its widget share a compact local summary, such as ride counts, available distance, time, calories, milestones and a suggestion. The widget does not receive raw heart-rate samples, route coordinates or private notes. People who can see your widget may see its totals. iOS controls widget refresh timing.

Sharing is your choice

Review your card and edit your caption before sharing. The card contains the visible ride distance, time and date, plus an optional chosen photo and optional active-calorie comparison. It leaves out route locations, heart rate and private notes. Captions contain only the text you approve. Temporary export files remain locally until they expire or are cleared.

The ordinary iOS share sheet transfers a copy to the destination you choose. The optional frgmnts handoff opens an editable draft; it never publishes automatically. A receiving app's privacy policy applies to its copy, and how many cannot recall cards already sent or published. The companion frgmnts service is separate from this journal.

Erasing and keeping a copy

In Settings, choose Erase this local journal to remove the open journal's imported data, notes, bike details, Readiness entries, counting choices, photo associations, private photo copies and saved widget summary. Apple Health and original Photos are unchanged. Sample and Health journals use separate storage.

Before deleting the app or erasing a journal, copy any important notes and keep original photos in Photos. Sharing a ride card is not a complete journal backup. Accessible Health workouts can be imported again; local additions cannot.

Support and this website

If you contact support, we receive the message and information you choose to send. Do not send private Health exports or exact routes unless they are necessary and you want to share them. This website's hosting and contact practices are described in the Outside The World privacy policy.

Privacy questions: ryandavid@outsidetheworld.com. For app help, visit how many support.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page and its effective date when the app's data practices change. The journal is intended for everyday cycling reflection; its unvalidated personal comparisons are not medical advice.
