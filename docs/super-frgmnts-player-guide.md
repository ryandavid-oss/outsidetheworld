## Welcome to Veyra

A distress signal has reached Aryn Sol-Mavi from a world the Fleet abandoned.
The Veyra Coreworks are poisoned, the atmospheric stabilizers are offline, and
something is moving through the Vesperite seams.

Your first assignment:

1. Land the RD-42 and locate Dras Ehdre.
2. Prepare Aryn's PACK aboard the ship.
3. Restore both atmospheric stabilizers.
4. Recover all 12 Vesperite Fragments.
5. Trace the pulse to its source.

The game saves episode progress in the current browser session. Use **Pause >
Restart Checkpoint** to retry a section. Use **Pause > Return to Title** for a
complete reset.

## Field Personnel

### Aryn Sol-Mavi - Signal Ranger

Aryn came to Veyra in her own ship and under her own authority. Her backpack
chassis begins with a guided base beam and accepts field-built modules as the
mission develops.

### Dras Ehdre - Coreworks Foreman

Dras is the last foreman still watching the abandoned Coreworks. Talk to him
whenever Aryn returns to the surface. He provides assignments, materials, and
an entirely reasonable concern about what is living below.

### RD-42

Aryn's ship is also her workshop. Stand at the labeled dorsal hatch and press
Down to enter. PACK upgrades must be installed at the ship bench.

## PS4 Controller

### Title screen

- **Cross:** Start the game.
- **Triangle:** Switch between Normal and Hard Mode.

### In the field

- **Left Stick or D-pad:** Move Aryn.
- **Down:** Interact, talk, enter hatches, activate stations, recover marked
  objective items, or drop through a catwalk.
- **Cross:** Jump. Release early for a shorter jump. After Jet Assist is found,
  press Cross again in midair for one boost.
- **Square, R1, or R2:** Fire the PACK beam. Hold when a charging module is
  active.
- **Triangle:** Open PACK Configuration after Ghost Vector has been installed.
- **Options:** Pause or resume.

### Menus and dialogue

- **D-pad or Left Stick:** Move the selection.
- **Cross:** Confirm the highlighted choice, continue, or toggle a PACK module.
- **Circle:** Back. During dialogue, Circle opens or closes the skip menu.
- **Triangle:** Move between the Pause menu and PACK Configuration.
- **Options:** Resume immediately.
- **Sound:** Pause, highlight **Sound**, and press Cross to toggle it.

When the dialogue skip confirmation appears, **Keep Listening** is selected
first. Use the D-pad or Left Stick to choose **Skip Scene**, then press Cross.

## Touch Controls

Play in landscape orientation.

- Drag the **left control disc** left or right to move.
- Pull the disc **down** to interact or drop through a catwalk.
- Tap **Jump** to jump. Tap it again in midair after Jet Assist is acquired.
- Tap or hold **Fire** to use the current PACK configuration.
- Tap **Pause**, then **PACK Configuration**, to change discovered modules.
- Tap buttons and dialogue cards directly to select or continue.

If the browser presents **Load Game**, press Cross or tap it once. Some browsers
may keep audio muted until the page receives a keyboard or touch activation,
but controller play is never blocked.

## Coreworks Basics

### Move, jump, and drop

Aryn's jump has variable height. Hold Cross or Jump for full height; release
early for a short hop. Press Down while standing on most catwalks to drop to
the deck below. Some solid floors contain marked Deepworks entry points.

### Interact

Press Down when a label appears over a person, hatch, machine, transport, or
special recovery item. Most loose pickups are collected automatically on
contact.

### Fire and collect

Shoot credit crates to break them open. Credits and health released by defeated
enemies fly directly to Aryn. Health drops are absorbed even when Energy is
already full.

### Watch the architecture

Mechanical catwalks are usually traversable. Painted background walkways are
not always solid. Zap platforms announce their active cycle with bright energy;
cross during the quiet interval.

## Mission Route

### 1. Arrival

Find Dras, receive the Ghost Vector material, return to the RD-42, and install
the upgrade at the PACK bench.

### 2. The Shard Foundry

Restore the Foundry and Biolab atmospheric stabilizers, recover all 12
Vesperite Fragments, and contain the roaming infestation. Clear the first
underground defenders to reveal Prism Splinter. Jet Assist becomes available
after the Foundry stabilizer is online.

### 3. The Wound

Reach the Uplink route and follow the pulse. Aryn's first Seam Hunter encounter
is part of the story; survival and observation matter more than victory.

### 4. Materials Vault

After reporting to Dras, use the portal to reach the three-level storage vault.
Fight upward and recover the Heliocline catalyst from secure storage.

### 5. Rematch

Install Solar Needle aboard the RD-42, return to Dras, and take the prepared
route back to the Wound.

## PACK Configuration

This is one modular backpack weapon, not a weapon inventory. Every enabled
module changes the same beam. Open PACK Configuration with Triangle after Ghost
Vector is installed. Modules can be toggled independently.

### Backpack Base

A fast guided shot. It tracks nearby targets and is the easiest configuration
to control.

### Ghost Vector

Passes through solid terrain and closed geometry. The extra phase speed removes
seeker guidance, so aim with Aryn's position.

### Prism Splinter

Divides the beam into three widening lanes. Excellent coverage, but the split
shots fly straight.

### Solar Needle

Adds high direct damage and enemy penetration. Guidance is substantially
reduced.

### Recommended combinations

- **Ghost only:** Safest way to attack through walls and platforms.
- **Prism only:** Broad crowd control in open rooms.
- **Prism + Solar:** Strong rematch configuration with wide penetrating fire.
- **Everything off:** Returns to the guided Backpack Base.

Changing the PACK clears any charged shot that was waiting to fire.

## Energy, Pickups, and Hazards

### Energy

Aryn begins with **12 Energy**, displayed as three bars of four points.

- **Health Cell:** Restores 1 Energy.
- **Health Core:** Restores 4 Energy.
- **Enemy drop:** Flies toward Aryn and is collected automatically.

After damage, Aryn briefly flashes and cannot be hit again until recovery ends.
At low Energy the HUD shifts from teal to amber, then magenta-red.

### Mission pickups

- **Vesperite Fragment:** Recover all 12 to satisfy the Uplink requirement.
- **Credit Crate:** Shoot it open, then collect the released Galactic Credits.
- **Jet Assist:** Grants one additional midair boost before landing.
- **Diet Coke / Prism Splinter:** Touch the can after clearing its defenders.
- **Heliocline Catalyst:** Press Down at the marked vault recovery point.

### Hazards

- **Zap platforms:** Cross during the quiet portion of the energy cycle.
- **Thermal vents:** Wait for the purge to stop before passing.
- **Seam creatures:** Some crawl around every side of a platform.
- **Locked bubble doors:** Restore the related stabilizer before crossing.

## Difficulty and Field Notes

### Normal Mode

Standard hostile integrity and Seam Hunter damage.

### Hard Mode

- Ordinary enemies have 3x their normal Energy.
- The Seam Hunter receives 200 additional Energy.
- The Seam Hunter's attacks inflict increased damage.

Choose the mode on the title screen with Triangle. Difficulty remains selected
for the current browser session.

### Field notes

- A green bubble door is ready. A red one still has an unmet objective.
- Health from defeated enemies is absorbed whether Aryn needs it or not.
- If a route looks unreachable, search for Jet Assist or a drop-through deck.
- If your beam is behaving strangely, open PACK and check which modules are lit.
- When the first Seam Hunter feels impossible, the game has noticed.
- If one particular bubble door appears to have moved again, it is now part of
  Veyran architectural tradition.
