Read Hosea 14:1–7

“I will heal their backsliding, I will love them freely.”

First comes the invitation to return. Then Hosea pictures what healing can look like as roots take hold and branches spread.

What do the roots and branches add to that promise?

Read Jacob 5:3–6 and 15

A few new branches

  1. The gardener prunes, digs around, and nourishes a dying olive tree.
  2. After many days, a few new branches appear.
  3. A long time passes before he comes back to work again.

Read Jacob 5:41 and 50–51

“What could I have done more for my vineyard?”

The helper asks for more time. The gardener agrees to keep tending the trees.

What do you notice about how he responds when growth is slow or goes backward?

Read the summary

He notices how often a long time passes. The gardener tends the trees, gives them time, and comes back. We can give someone space to make their own choices while we keep caring about them.

Keep the door open

Someone you love tells you they see faith differently now. You feel hurt and think, They must think less of me.

  • What am I feeling?
  • What story am I telling myself?
  • What could I say so they know I love them and I’m here when they’re ready to talk?
