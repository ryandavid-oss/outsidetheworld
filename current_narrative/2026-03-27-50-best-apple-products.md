# 50 Best Apple Products
Date: March 27, 2026

A wonderful bit of nostalgia-bait posted to The Verge today.  And they did it in the best way possible: a pitch-perfect recreation of the Classic Mac OS. I wish I had more experience with the products the staff chose; items like the black MacBook, PowerBooks, and Bondi Blue iMac took me right back to working at Best Buy in the 1990s. 

[Go ahead and check it out here.](https://www.theverge.com/cs/tech/900477/apple-50-anniversary-rank-products)

Don't be surprised if you get paralyzed between choices like I did with this one. How on earth could I pick between such astonishing technologies that changed the way I computed forever?
